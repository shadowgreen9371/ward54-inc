Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Ward 54 INC — Kolkata mobile application (the “App”) operated by the Ward 54 unit of the Indian National Congress, Kolkata Municipal Corporation, handles your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal information about you. Specifically:

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, address, Aadhaar, EPIC number, or any other personal identifier.
  • We do not use any analytics SDK (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, etc.).
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We do not have access to your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, or location.
  • We do not share any data with third parties because no data is collected in the first place.

2. What the App actually does

The App is a thin wrapper around our public website at https://kolkata54ward.in/. When you open the App, it loads the same publicly available information that anyone could view in a web browser:

  • Public voter list for Ward 54 (already published by the Election Commission of India).
  • Polling station locations and booth information.
  • Names and roles of party office bearers who have consented to public listing.
  • Public meeting notices and announcements.

3. Voter list disclaimer

The voter list shown in the App is the official electoral roll published by the Election Commission of India under public-disclosure norms. We display it for the convenience of Ward 54 residents. If you believe your entry should be updated, please contact your nearest Booth Level Officer (BLO) or visit https://www.nvsp.in/.

4. Admin features

Some screens of the App are gated behind an admin password and are intended only for Ward 54 INC office bearers. Admin authentication is performed entirely on your own device — your password is never transmitted to any server controlled by us. Admin actions write data back to our public GitHub repository using a personal access token that the admin manually configures on their device; we do not store, log, or process that token.

5. Cookies & local storage

The website that the App loads uses your device's local storage to remember UI preferences (such as the last-opened section). This data never leaves your device and is automatically cleared when you uninstall the App or clear app data.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adult voters and party workers. We do not knowingly direct the App to children under 13 and we do not collect information from anyone.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy we will update the “Last updated” date at the top and post the new version at the same URL. Material changes will also be announced inside the App on the Digital Notice Board.
